# Cover Style Guide

## Goal

Standardize the default album-cover direction for longform music-platform songs so covers feel:

- comfortable
- healing
- gently uplifting
- clean and replay-friendly

This guide is especially for:

- `qishui_music`
- `fanqie_music`
- emotionally warm, replay-oriented singles

## Core Direction

The preferred visual direction is:

- soft Japanese-inspired illustration
- hand-painted texture
- calm and elegant composition
- quiet but not depressed
- uplifting but not overly bright or childish

The emotional target is:

- the character looks emotionally settled or gradually getting better
- the image feels like being gently received by light, air, morning, or a quiet room
- the overall mood should make the viewer feel relaxed, safe, and willing to listen

## Character Preference

When a person appears on the cover, prefer:

- calm
- clear-eyed
- gentle
- lightly optimistic
- reflective but not broken

Avoid:

- despair
- collapse
- aggressive crying
- overly dramatic sorrow
- cold fashion-editorial detachment

## Color Preference

Preferred palette:

- pale blue
- dawn light
- soft white
- cream
- light gold
- gray-blue
- low-saturation plant green

The image should feel:

- airy
- breathable
- clean
- soft

Avoid color directions that feel:

- neon urban
- cyberpunk
- muddy
- over-contrasted
- nightclub-like

## Scene Preference

Preferred scene families:

- window-side room
- before-sunrise or early-morning light
- quiet indoor corner
- gentle plants
- calm sky
- distant hills or soft landscape
- subtle desk objects or daily-life details

These elements work well:

- window frame
- cup
- notebook
- curtains
- soft chair or bed corner
- indoor plants
- warm reflected light

## Hard Avoid List

Do not default to:

- neon city streets
- subway cars
- commercial signboards
- cyberpunk nightlife
- phone close-ups
- harsh commute-night visuals
- dark cinematic realism
- poster-like ad composition
- chaotic cluttered rooms

## Platform Rationale

This direction works well for Qishui and Fanqie because:

- it feels emotionally approachable
- it supports replayable full-song listening
- it looks less like a trend-chasing short-video asset
- it pairs naturally with healing, reflective, and lightly uplifting songs

## Practical Rule

If the song is not explicitly dark, angry, or rebellious, the default cover should lean toward:

- healing
- calm
- gentle upward energy

When in doubt, choose:

- cleaner
- softer
- more breathable

instead of:

- louder
- darker
- more urban
